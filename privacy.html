SquadGoo is an Australian marketplace operated by SquadGoo Pty Ltd. Business users create job offers (Manual Fill and Quick Fill) and match KYC-verified jobseekers (and eligible Service Providers). Customers post task-style help requests via Match Maker. We are not a traditional job board of open applications. 1. Who we are

“SquadGoo”, “we”, “us” means SquadGoo Pty Ltd (Australia). You can use SquadGoo as a:

  • Jobseeker — receive Manual / Quick offers and complete shifts;
  • Business — Company / Enterprises or Sole Trader / Small Business, operating in Recruiter mode (Find Staff / Hiring Active) or Service Provider mode (receive offers / Match Maker pool when verified and eligible);
  • Customer — post Match Maker Manual / Quick task requests and invite helpers from the Provider Pool.

Some people switch account types or Business profile mode on the same login. Company wallets stay separate from personal wallets. Personal Recruiter and Jobseeker modes may share a personal wallet when product rules allow.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account & profile

  • Name, email, mobile number, password or social sign-in tokens (Apple / Google where enabled).
  • Account type / role, Business subtype (company vs sole trader), Business profile mode (recruiter vs service provider), display ID, referral codes.
  • Profile photo, bio, education, work experience (drafts/published), references and consent flags, skills, qualifications and expiry dates.
  • Visa / work-rights declarations and related document uploads (profile eligibility — separate from KYC identity checks).
  • Address or location pin and distance preferences used for matching.
  • Shift / Service preferences (channels, job types, pay ranges, weekday availability windows).
  • Company profile fields for company Business accounts (legal/trading name, ABN/ACN, industry, logo, representative details, company social links).
  • Customer Match Maker request fields (task description, schedule, place, budget, shortlists / invites).

2.2 Identity verification (KYC / KYB)

  • Identity documents, selfie / liveness outcomes, and verification status for KYC.
  • For company Business accounts only: Know Your Business (KYB) documents and verification status.
  • Verification outcomes, staff review notes, and resubmission history.

KYC / KYB document images and files are for SquadGoo compliance staff only. Other app users (jobseekers, recruiters, customers, public profiles) do not receive those document assets — even after an offer is accepted. Accepting an offer may unlock contacts and certain non-KYC attachments under contact-window rules; it does not unlock identity KYC/KYB document files.

2.3 Tax & business registry

  • Tax fields you provide (e.g. ABN and/or TFN) used to gate Quick Fill / Manual Fill eligibility (Pay via SquadGoo / SquadGoo Guaranteed requires a valid ABN for jobseekers; Pay direct and Manual Fill follow the poster’s tax-holder preference).
  • ABN/ACN lookup results from the Australian Business Register (ABR) during signup or company verification.
  • We treat TFN and similar identifiers as sensitive; access is limited and used for eligibility and compliance workflows.

2.4 Offers, shifts, Match Maker & operations

  • Jobs, Customer Match requests, and offers you create or receive (Manual Fill, Quick Fill — Pay via SquadGoo / Direct Pay), modification requests, accept/decline history, acceptance rating.
  • Shift lifecycle events: DEPART, live GPS during allowed windows, Arrived, arrival codes, clock in/out, pause/resume, Adjust Shift Ends, key tasks and day notes (including optional media) for Guaranteed shifts.
  • Cancellations, leave/kick (Manual Fill), stop-filling / reopen positions, conflict and grace-window checks.
  • Invoices and payout hold records for Guaranteed shifts; dispute chat and finance settlement records when raised.

2.5 Communications

  • In-app job / offer chat, dispute chat (with SquadGoo staff), support tickets / live chat, push and email/SMS notifications (including OTP codes).
  • Public Forum posts, comments, reactions/votes, shares, reports and blocks.
  • SquadPairs group membership, invites, join requests, and accepted SquadPairs Terms version.
  • Reviews you write or receive (one lifetime review per author→subject pair; editable).

2.6 Payments & wallet

  • SG Coin (SquadCoin) balances, holds, matchmaking fees, salary escrow, payout windows, withdrawals, referral / bonus buckets (bonus coins may be non-withdrawable).
  • Payment provider metadata (e.g. Stripe customer / PaymentIntent IDs, PayID deposit references). Card numbers are handled by Stripe — we do not store full card PAN on SquadGoo servers.

2.7 Device, diagnostics & analytics

  • Device type, OS version, app version, IP address, approximate network info.
  • Crash / error reports (e.g. Sentry where configured) and limited product analytics to keep the service reliable.
  • Push notification tokens (Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging where configured).

2.8 Admin / staff operations

When you interact with support or compliance, authorised Owner/Staff users may access the same underlying records (User 360, disputes, KYC/KYB, offers, Match Maker) under permission keys. Staff portal accounts are real operations accounts.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts; send OTP / verification codes; prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Match jobseekers, Service Providers, recruiters, and Customers; score matches; enforce availability, tax, visa, and qualification rules.
  • Run Manual Fill and Quick Fill offer flows (recruiter and Customer Match Maker), modifications, chat unlock, and contact windows.
  • Operate shift tracking, codes, clocking, escrow, invoices, 7-day payout holds, and dispute resolution for Pay via SquadGoo.
  • Show Live Pulse aggregates (rounded, privacy-preserving platform heartbeat — not a directory of who is hiring).
  • Power Public Forum, SquadPairs, reviews, notifications, and customer support.
  • Comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and protect users and the platform.
  • Improve product quality and safety (including reviewing reported content).

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Location & shift tracking

Location is used for matching (preference pin / radius) and, for Quick Fill accepted shifts, for operational tracking after you grant permission and tap DEPART.

  • Direct Pay: GPS tracking typically stops when you mark Arrived.
  • Pay via SquadGoo (SquadGoo Guaranteed): jobseeker location may continue until clock-out so operations/admin can support the shift; recruiter live-map UI still ends at Arrived.
  • You can deny location permission; some features (DEPART / live tracking) will not work without it.
  • Optional cancel-shift location may be captured if you grant permission at cancel time.

5. Payments, wallet & invoices

  • Recruiters fund matchmaking fees and, for Guaranteed offers, salary escrow in SG Coins (1 SG Coin ≈ AUD 1).
  • Jobseeker Guaranteed payouts are held (typically 7 days from shift completion) before wallet credit unless a dispute freezes release.
  • Customer Match Maker MVP invites are Direct-style (no matchmaking fee / wallet hold) unless product messaging states otherwise for a later Guaranteed path.
  • Disputes may involve chat with both parties and SquadGoo staff; Guaranteed money movement follows finance/accounts approval — not an instant chat-side wallet change.
  • Labour invoices for Guaranteed shifts are labour-only and state that no GST has been charged on that jobseeker labour invoice. Tax outcomes for your situation may differ — seek your own advice.

6. Forum, chat, reviews & SquadPairs

  • Public Forum: browse/read may be available when signed in; creating posts, reacting, commenting, sharing, or going live may require KYC. Content you post or share may be visible per visibility settings (including private shares visible to you and moderation staff).
  • Profile privacy: Settings → Privacy Controls → “Allow Public Forum profile view” can limit expanded profile views (author chips on posts remain visible).
  • Active / Online status: marketplace availability and approximate online presence may appear on public identity chips where the product shows other users.
  • Job / offer chat: unlocked under contact-window rules after accept; may close on leave/kick/cancel while contacts can remain visible for a limited window.
  • Reviews: associated with your account and visible where product surfaces show ratings.
  • SquadPairs: membership and role slots are stored; joining requires acceptance of versioned SquadPairs Terms.
  • Profile views (jobseekers): when a recruiter opens your full profile, we may notify you with a masked viewer label (not full contact details).

7. Sharing & processors

We share personal information only as needed to run the service, including:

  • Counterparties: after offer accept, limited contact and shift details with the other party for the contact window; Manual Fill leave/kick and Quick cancel rules may still show contacts for a limited time while chat is locked.
  • Company peer contacts: in limited announcement contexts, company Business users may see another company’s contact details; Public Forum company profile views keep contacts masked.
  • Payment processors: e.g. Stripe for card top-ups / payments; bank PayID rails for deposits where offered.
  • Apple / Google: App Store / Play billing or sign-in services when you use those features; push delivery via APNs / FCM.
  • ABR: Australian Business Register lookups to verify ABN/ACN / entity names you submit.
  • Communications: SMS/email/push providers (e.g. Twilio / Microsoft 365 SMTP where configured) for OTP and notifications.
  • Cloud hosting & storage: e.g. AWS (API, file uploads), database hosting (e.g. MongoDB Atlas).
  • Error monitoring: e.g. Sentry where configured.
  • Maps / places: map and Places SDK providers when you use address / location features.
  • Law & safety: when required by law or to investigate fraud, harm, or Terms breaches.
  • Staff: authorised operations staff under RBAC permission keys.

8. Privacy controls in the app

  • Show Live Pulse on Home (on/off) — Dashboard Live Pulse feed may still show platform status.
  • Allow Public Forum profile view (on/off).
  • Open to SquadPairs invites (jobseekers; default on).
  • Notification preferences (where offered).
  • OS-level permission controls for location, camera, photos, microphone, notifications.
  • Account deletion / data export requests via in-app settings or support where available.

9. Security & retention

We use industry-standard controls appropriate to a hosted marketplace (encryption in transit, access controls, segmented staff permissions). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

We retain information as long as your account is active and as needed for offers, payouts, disputes, tax/accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Some records (e.g. financial ledgers, dispute files, verification audits) are kept longer even after account closure when we must.

10. Your rights & account deletion

Under the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), you may request access to and correction of personal information we hold. Contact us using the details below or via in-app support.

You may request account deletion. We will delete or de-identify personal data where we are not required to retain it. Deletion or anonymisation may be delayed while an open dispute, payout hold, unpaid obligation, or compliance review is active. Content you posted publicly may remain in anonymised or “unavailable” form where needed for integrity of threads.

11. Children

SquadGoo is for adults with work rights. It is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

12. International transfers

Infrastructure and processors may store or process data in Australia and other countries (for example cloud regions used by AWS, Stripe, Apple, Google, or monitoring tools). Where we transfer personal information overseas, we take steps consistent with APP 8, including using reputable providers and contractual protections where appropriate.
