Agreement

This Privacy Policy explains how SquadGoo collects, uses, discloses, and stores personal information when you use the SquadGoo mobile application, website, and related services in Australia. It is written to align with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By creating an account or using SquadGoo, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where we need consent for a specific purpose (for example certain marketing messages), we will ask for it separately in-app. In-app acknowledgement is intended to have effect under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth) and corresponding State and Territory laws.

1. Who we are

SquadGoo operates a digital marketplace connecting:

  • Jobseekers seeking shift-based work
  • Business users (Company / Enterprises and Sole Trader / Small Business) acting as recruiters and/or service providers
  • Customers posting task-style Match Maker requests

We provide matching, offers (Manual Fill and Quick Fill), shift tracking, wallets (SG Coins), chat, Public Forum, KYC/KYB verification workflows, and related support tools. SquadGoo is a technology platform intermediary — not an employer of jobseekers by default, and not a party to every off-platform arrangement after Manual Fill accept or Off Platform Pay code handoff (as described in our Terms of Use).

Contact: support@squadgoo.com.au · In-app Help & Support · Web: https://www.squadgoo.com.au

2. What personal information we collect

Depending on your account type and features you use, we may collect:

2.1 Identity and account

  • Name, email, mobile number, password or social sign-in identifiers (Google / Apple where enabled)
  • Display ID (for example JS-, CU-, company identifiers)
  • Profile photo, bio, and public profile fields you choose to show
  • Date of birth or age indicators where required for eligibility
  • Device identifiers, push tokens, and session / security metadata

2.2 Verification (KYC / KYB)

  • Identity document details and images (passport, licence, selfie / liveness) for verification
  • Address and address proof where requested
  • Company / business registration details, ABN/ACN, ABR lookup results, registration proof, representative details (company KYB; light KYB for Sole Trader where applicable)

Important product rule: KYC/KYB document assets are for SquadGoo admin/staff verification only. Mobile counterparties (other jobseekers, recruiters, customers) do not receive identity KYC/KYB document files — even after an offer is accepted. Contact unlock is separate from KYC document access.

2.3 Tax and work rights

  • ABN and/or TFN details you enter in Tax settings (handled with heightened care)
  • Visa / work-rights declarations and related uploads (profile work rights — distinct from KYC identity docs)

2.4 Marketplace and shift data

  • Shift preferences, availability, experience, qualifications, education
  • Job / Match Maker request details, offers, acceptances, declines, modification requests
  • Location data when you grant permission (for example DEPART / live GPS tracking, Maps pin for preferences or Customer Match Maker requests)
  • Arrival codes, clock in/out, pause periods, key tasks and notes, invoices metadata
  • Acceptance rating, Hire Reliability, and related standing metrics

2.5 Payments and wallet

  • SG Coin balances, holds, escrow, top-ups, withdrawals, referral bonuses
  • Payment processor tokens / references (for example Stripe) — we do not store full card PAN where the processor holds it
  • Bank / payout details you provide for withdrawals

2.6 Communications and community

  • In-app chat messages, support tickets, dispute chat content and attachments
  • Public Forum posts, comments, reactions, shares, reports
  • Notifications preferences and delivery logs (push, in-app, email where enabled)
  • Call metadata where voice features are used

2.7 Technical and analytics

  • App version, device model, OS, crash / performance diagnostics (for example Sentry)
  • Approximate network information and security logs (login, OTP, device approvals for staff portals where applicable)

We may also collect information you provide to support staff, or that is reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

3. How we collect information

  • Directly from you (forms, uploads, chat, toggles)
  • Automatically from your device and the app (subject to permissions)
  • From identity / business verification providers and ABR-style lookups you initiate
  • From payment processors and messaging providers acting as our service providers
  • From other users only as needed for marketplace operation (for example offer parties, masked labels until contact unlock)

We aim not to collect sensitive information unless reasonably necessary (for example identity verification photos) and permitted under the APPs.

4. Why we use personal information (purposes)

We use personal information to:

  • Create and secure accounts; authenticate users (including OTP / social sign-in)
  • Match jobseekers / helpers with recruiters / customers; score and display match information
  • Operate Manual Fill, Quick Fill, Customer Match Maker, SquadPairs (where enabled), and related flows
  • Process matchmaking fees, escrow, payouts, invoices, refunds, and disputes
  • Provide shift tracking (DEPART, GPS where permitted, arrival codes, clock)
  • Enable chat and contact windows after accept, subject to product rules
  • Moderate Public Forum and enforce Community Guidelines / Forum Terms
  • Verify KYC/KYB and eligibility gates (Receiving Offers, Hiring Active, tax / visa preferences)
  • Send transactional notifications; send marketing only where permitted / consented
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents; comply with law and respond to lawful requests
  • Improve product quality, safety, and customer support
  • Meet record-keeping and audit obligations

5. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Other users — limited profile and offer fields as the product shows (contacts and certain documents only after unlock rules; KYC docs never to counterparties)
  • Service providers — hosting, databases, email/SMS, push, maps, payments, analytics/crash, AI assistance features, identity verification vendors — under contracts requiring appropriate handling
  • Company Field Pass assignees — limited job-scoped details when a Company Service Provider assigns field staff (as product rules describe)
  • Professional advisers and insurers where reasonably necessary
  • Regulators, courts, or law enforcement where required or authorised by law
  • A successor entity in a business transfer, with notice where required

We do not sell personal information for a fee in the ordinary advertising sense. We do not provide KYC identity document files to mobile counterparties.

5.1 Public Live Pulse activity

If you choose to show your activity publicly, marketplace events you take part in — such as an interview request, an accepted offer, a published job ad, or a Match Maker scan — may appear in the public Live Pulse feed under your real name for other SquadGoo users to see. When an activity involved a fee, the feed may show an approximate fee range rather than the exact amount. It does not show your contact details.

You can keep your activity private or turn public activity off later in Settings → Privacy Controls. Turning it off removes your past and future activity from the public feed. Turning it back on restores visibility only while your acknowledgement of the current policy is valid. The separate Show Live Pulse on Home setting changes only whether you see Live Pulse on your own Home screen; it does not make your activity public.

6. Overseas disclosure

Our infrastructure or vendors may process data in Australia and other countries (for example cloud regions used by hosting, payments, messaging, or analytics providers). Where APP 8 applies, we take reasonable steps to ensure overseas recipients handle personal information consistently with the APPs, or otherwise rely on permitted exceptions (including your consent where appropriate). Ask support@squadgoo.com.au for current primary processing locations.

7. Storage and security

We use administrative, technical, and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information (access controls, encryption in transit, staff permission keys for admin tools, monitoring). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Please use a strong unique password and protect your device.

8. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, accounting, dispute, and safety retention periods. KYC artefacts, financial records, and dispute materials may be kept longer where required. When no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify information.

9. Access, correction, and your choices

Under the Privacy Act you may request access to, and correction of, personal information we hold about you (subject to permitted exceptions). Use in-app profile tools where available, or email support@squadgoo.com.au.

You may also:

  • Update profile, tax, visa, and notification preferences in Settings
  • Control certain privacy toggles (for example Live Pulse on Home, Public Forum profile view)
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing where consent is the basis (this may limit features)
  • Request account closure subject to outstanding obligations (escrow, disputes, legal holds)

10. Children

SquadGoo is directed to adults capable of forming binding contracts under Australian law (generally 18+). We do not knowingly create full marketplace accounts for children. Contact us if you believe we hold a child's information inappropriately.

11. Direct marketing

We may send service messages without marketing consent. Marketing communications (where used) will include an unsubscribe or preference path and comply with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) where applicable.

12. Automated decision-making and AI

Matching scores, fraud signals, Forum pre-checks, and AI drafting assistants may use automated processing. Significant eligibility or enforcement decisions that affect you can be reviewed via Help & Support. AI outputs are assistive; you remain responsible for content you publish and information you submit.

13. Complaints

  1. Email support@squadgoo.com.au with details and your contact information.
  2. We will acknowledge and investigate within a reasonable time.
  3. If unresolved, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) — https://www.oaic.gov.au

We may update this Privacy Policy by publishing a new version in-app (Policy Centre / PDF) with a new version and effective date. Material changes will be highlighted where practical. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acknowledgement of the updated policy where permitted by law.

15. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is intended for Australian users and is interpreted alongside Australian privacy law. Platform contracts are otherwise governed as stated in the Terms of Use (typically New South Wales / Commonwealth of Australia).
