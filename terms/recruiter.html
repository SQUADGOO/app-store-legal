4. Offers, matching, and availability

Manual Fill

Recruiter browses matched candidates and sends offers. After jobseeker accept, matchmaking fee is deducted and platform role typically ends (off-platform thereafter), subject to contact-window and other residual rules.

Quick Fill

System may auto-send to active eligible candidates for shifts starting within the product window (including up to 7 days ahead). Paths include Pay via SquadGoo (Guaranteed) and Pay direct (Off Platform Pay).

Availability and grace

Accepted-shift buffers, travel grace, accept grace, overlap blocks, and worked-hours caps apply as implemented in the app and APIs. Availability preferences are a hard matching gate.

Customer Match Maker

Customer requests use the Match Maker model (not a recruiter Job document). Guaranteed Customer paths may hold Match Maker fee + task pay escrow; Direct paths follow Direct rules. Quotes product is not part of the current marketplace.
