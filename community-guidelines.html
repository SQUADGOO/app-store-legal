Agreement

These Community Guidelines set behavioural standards for everyone using SquadGoo — Jobseekers, Business users (Recruiters and Service Providers — Sole Trader and Company), and Customers. They sit alongside our Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Public Forum Terms & Policies.

By using SquadGoo you agree to follow these Guidelines.

1. Our community standard

SquadGoo is a professional Australian marketplace for work and services. Treat people with respect. Compete fairly. Do not use the platform to harm, deceive, or exploit others.

2. Who these guidelines apply to

  • Jobseekers receiving and performing shifts
  • Business recruiters hiring staff (Manual Fill / Quick Fill)
  • Business Service Providers receiving Customer Match Maker requests
  • Customers posting Match Maker requests and managing helpers
  • Anyone using Public Forum, chat, reviews, or support channels

3. Be honest

  • Keep profiles, experience, qualifications, tax, and visa declarations accurate
  • Do not misrepresent company affiliation, verification badges, or availability
  • Job and Match Maker details must be truthful (pay, location, hours, scope)
  • Do not create fake accounts, duplicate accounts to evade bans, or manipulate match % / ratings

4. Be respectful and safe

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, stalking, or discrimination
  • No sexual misconduct, non-consensual imagery, or exploitation of minors
  • No doxxing or sharing others' private information without a lawful basis
  • Follow site safety rules when attending shifts; raise safety concerns via Help & Support

5. Marketplace integrity

  • Do not game availability, grace windows, or cancel/decline rules to abuse rating systems
  • Do not solicit off-platform payment to evade matchmaking fees or escrow protections where that breaches Terms
  • Direct Pay still requires honest completion of arrival / code steps; Guaranteed paths require honest clock and task records
  • Do not spam invites, scrape profiles, or automate abusive bulk actions

6. Communications

  • Use in-app chat professionally within contact windows
  • No scams, phishing, malware links, or deceptive recruitment
  • Reviews must be genuine and not misleading under the Australian Consumer Law
  • Public Forum content must also comply with Public Forum Terms & Policies

7. Illegal and prohibited activity

Do not use SquadGoo for illegal work, money laundering, fraud, identity theft, IP infringement, or any criminal purpose under Australian law.

8. Enforcement

We may warn, restrict features, remove content, suspend Receiving Offers / Hiring Active, or suspend/terminate accounts. Serious cases may be reported to authorities. Appeals: Help & Support or support@squadgoo.com.au.

9. Reporting

Report abuse, safety issues, and illegal content using in-app report tools or Help & Support. Bad-faith reporting may itself be enforced against.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these Guidelines by publishing a new version in-app / PDF. Contact: support@squadgoo.com.au · or in-app Help & Support.
