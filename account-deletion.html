Delete your SquadGoo account

This page explains how a SquadGoo account holder can start deletion and what may prevent immediate completion.

Start in the app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings, then Delete Account.
  3. Review the permanent-deletion notice and confirm using the account-security step shown in the app.

You can also open the direct web account-deletion resource or contact support@squadgoo.com.au if you cannot access the app.

When deletion can complete

An eligible, confirmed request is processed immediately by the account-deletion service. The request can be refused while protected obligations remain, including an open shift or task, held funds, an unresolved dispute, or another legal or safety hold. Resolve the stated obligation and submit the request again.

Data deletion and retention

SquadGoo deletes or de-identifies account data that is no longer required. Some transaction, dispute, safety, tax, legal or audit records may be retained where the law or a protected obligation requires it. Public Forum or marketplace records that must remain for thread, transaction or audit integrity are de-identified so the deleted account is no longer presented as the active author.

Help

For deletion, access or correction questions, contact support@squadgoo.com.au. Do not email passwords, verification codes, identity documents or payment details.
