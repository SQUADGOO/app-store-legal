1. Who we are and what the Public Forum is

1.1 Platform role

SquadGoo operates a digital marketplace and community platform connecting jobseekers, business users (recruiters and service providers), and customers for shift-based work and related services in Australia. The Public Forum is an in-app community surface for employment-related stories, news, general posts, discussions, Job Ads (interest-only discovery), reactions, comments, shares, and (where enabled) live sessions.

SquadGoo is a technology platform intermediary. We are not the publisher of user-generated content in the ordinary sense of selecting and endorsing every post as our own editorial content. We host, transmit, moderate, and may remove content under these Forum Terms and applicable law. We do not guarantee that any post, comment, or live stream is accurate, complete, or lawful.

1.2 Relationship to hiring and marketplace features

Public Forum activity is separate from Manual Fill / Quick Fill offers, Customer Match Maker, wallets, and shift lifecycle. Forum posts do not create job offers, employment contracts, or payment obligations by themselves. Job Ads are discovery posts only: Apply Now records interest for the poster to review in Labor Pool or Provider Pool — it is not an offer, application to a job board, or shift booking. Posters send real Manual/Quick offers separately after reviewing applicants. Contact unlock, KYC/KYB document access, and offer rules remain governed by the general product terms and in-app rules for those features.

You must be capable of forming a binding contract under Australian law (generally 18+, or as otherwise permitted with guardian consent where the product allows). You must not use the Forum if you are suspended from it or from the platform.
